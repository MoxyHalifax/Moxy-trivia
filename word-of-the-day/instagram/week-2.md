### Sun Nov 15: serendipity
```
This word was invented in 1754, inspired by a fairy tale. 🏰

Today's word: serendipity (sair-en-DIP-ih-tee)
👉 finding something good by lucky chance

Writer Horace Walpole made it up after an old Persian tale, The Three Princes of Serendip, who kept making lucky discoveries. Serendip is an old name for Sri Lanka.

Try this tonight: ask your student about their luckiest find ever. Teachers, it's a perfect creative-writing prompt.
```

### Tue Nov 17: lethargic
```
Tired teen on a Monday morning? There's a word for that. 😴

Today's word: lethargic (luh-THAR-jik)
👉 tired, slow, and lacking energy

It goes back to Greek myth and the river Lethe. One sip made you forget everything and feel sleepy.
```

### Thu Nov 19: scrutinize
```
This word might come from digging through a pile of junk. 🔍

From the Latin scrutari, to search, possibly from scruta: rags or junk.

Try this tonight: have your student scrutinize their last assignment for one mistake before handing it in. Teachers, it's the perfect word for editing day.
```
