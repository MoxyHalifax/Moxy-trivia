### Day 1: meticulous
Word of the day: **meticulous** (muh-TIK-yuh-lus) — showing great attention to detail.
Fun fact: it comes from the Latin word for *fear*. 🧐
Ask your student to use it at dinner tonight.

### Day 2: resilient
Word of the day: **resilient** (rih-ZIL-yent) — able to bounce back after something hard happens.
It comes from Latin for *to leap back*. 💪
Where has your student been resilient this year?
#wordoftheday #vocabulary #grade7 #grade8 #grade9 #middleschool #growthmindset #homeschool

### Day 3: ambiguous
Word of the day: **ambiguous** (am-BIG-yoo-us) — having more than one possible meaning.
Great word for book reports and movie talks. 🎬
#wordoftheday #vocabulary #grade7 #grade8 #grade9 #middleschool #englishclass #homeschool

### Day 4: benevolent
Word of the day: **benevolent** (buh-NEV-uh-lent) — kind and wanting to help others.
Latin *bene* (well) + *volens* (wishing) = wishing others well. ❄️
#wordoftheday #vocabulary #grade7 #grade8 #grade9 #middleschool #kindness #homeschool

### Day 5: procrastinate
Word of the day: **procrastinate** (proh-KRAS-tih-nayt) — to keep putting off something you should do.
Latin for *pushing things to tomorrow*. Sound familiar? 😅
Tag someone who needs this one.
#wordoftheday #vocabulary #grade7 #grade8 #grade9 #middleschool #studytips #homeschool

### Day 6: tenacious
Word of the day: **tenacious** (tuh-NAY-shus) — refusing to give up.
From Latin *tenere*, to hold. 🐶
#wordoftheday #vocabulary #grade7 #grade8 #grade9 #middleschool #learnenglish #homeschool

### Day 7: ubiquitous
