```
Ever had to be home by a certain time? That rule has a name from the Middle Ages. 🔔

You voted, so here it is! Today's word: curfew (KUR-fyoo)
👉 a set time when you must be home

It comes from the old French couvre feu: "cover the fire." A bell rang at night telling everyone to put out their fires and go to bed.

Try this tonight: ask your student what they think a fair curfew is, and why. Teachers, it's a great persuasive-writing prompt.
```

### Quarantine (Reel 014)
```
Ever had to stay home sick so no one else caught it? There's a word for that. 🤒

Today's word: quarantine (KWOR-un-teen)
👉 a time when people or animals are kept apart to stop a disease from spreading

It comes from the Italian quaranta giorni: forty days. Long ago in Venice, ships waited forty days before anyone could come ashore.

Try this tonight: ask your student why they think it was forty days. Teachers, it connects perfectly to science and social studies.
```

### Wed Nov 25: empathy (Reel 016)
```
Ever felt sad just because your friend was sad? There's a word for that. 💛

Today's word: empathy (EM-puh-thee)
👉 the ability to understand and share someone else's feelings

Greek: em (in) + pathos (feeling). It only became an English word about a hundred years ago.

Try this tonight: ask your student about a time someone showed them empathy. Teachers, it's a perfect fit for any kindness or SEL lesson.
```
